# The closing bell, drawn in code

The Afterhours interface now follows the real exchange clock. While the market is open the page
is a daylit art deco exchange; when the bell rings, the bell in the pediment swings, the sky goes
to dusk, the building turns to silhouette, and its windows light floor by floor into night. Every
drawing is SVG written in code, and every colour comes from one day palette and one night palette
mixed by a single animated number.

We checked it at three screen widths in day, night and mid-bell, fixed what looked wrong (text
briefly lost contrast mid-sequence, so the words now switch fast while the picture takes its
time), and measured the landing page at 94 for performance and 100 for accessibility.
