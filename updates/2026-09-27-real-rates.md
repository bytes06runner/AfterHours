# The real rate, next to the modelled one

Our backtest assumes lenders earn 7% to 9.5% in Stock Token markets. We checked the real ones.
On Robinhood Chain today, 150 Morpho markets take a Stock Token as collateral; lenders have
supplied 804,926 USDG and borrowers have taken 6,182, so lenders earn 0.0016%. The README, the
pitch and the report card now say that plainly next to every modelled yield.

We also counted how often our vault actually acts: on the test years it pulled money back on
228 nights, mostly before META and NVDA earnings. And the site got a proper entrance: the
exchange doors open when the page is ready, and the city lights up at night.
