# One command, one closing bell

`make demo` now brings up the whole stack on a local chain (clearly labelled Simulation) and
replays a real week from August 2026. On Tuesday nothing moves. At Wednesday's check, two closes
before the weekend, SPY's calibrated bad case plus our safety margin no longer fits the 91.5%
tier, so the bot moves the part of SPY's money that is not lent out to safer markets, and logs
the reason onchain. An automated test runs this unattended in under a minute.

We also fixed two things the demo exposed: the bot was chasing tiny rate differences, and some
reason cards named the wrong cause. Each card now says whether risk, rates or limits moved it.
