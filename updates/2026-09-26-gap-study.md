# How often the open gaps down

We measured every overnight, weekend, holiday and earnings night for 523 US stocks and ETFs
since 2010: just over two million closed periods, using split and dividend adjusted prices.

Earnings nights are a different animal. About 1 in 11 opens 5% or more below the prior close,
and about 1 in 50 opens 10% or more below. Weekends are calmer than that but still fatter tailed
than ordinary weeknights: a 10% gap down happens about 4.7 times as often after a weekend.

Those are the hours when a Stock Token keeps trading and its oracle does not. Next: a model that
predicts the bad case for each stock and each closed period, and proves it is calibrated.
