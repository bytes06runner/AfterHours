# Live tools for Stock Token borrowers and lenders

Afterhours' vault is still a simulation, but three things now read Robinhood Chain mainnet
directly, read-only, for anyone:

- A risk board with every Stock Token: when its price feed last moved, whether it is frozen for
  the weekend right now, and tonight's forecast bad case against each lending market's cushion.
- A position checker: paste any address or connect a wallet and see each Morpho loan's
  loan-to-value, the price that would liquidate it, and whether tonight's bad case gets there.
- A Telegram bot that follows a stock or an address and messages before the close only when
  tonight looks risky for it.

Nothing is signed and no funds move. Times show in New York and in your own time zone.
