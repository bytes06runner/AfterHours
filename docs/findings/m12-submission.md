# Colosseum submission requirements (M12)

Source: Colosseum hackathon FAQ, "What is the required information in the submission portal?",
https://colosseum.com/hackathon#faqs, read 2026-09-27. Deadline from
https://colosseum.com/worldsfair: "Submissions due October 12, 2026". Official rules PDF linked
there: https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World's%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf (not read).

The portal asks for (paraphrased from the FAQ list):

1. Product name and a brief description.
2. Which blockchains and tools are integrated.
3. All teammates, with backgrounds and previous experience (each teammate needs a Colosseum
   account; the team leader adds them during submission).
4. Where the team is located.
5. A product logo or graphic.
6. A GitHub repository link (open source encouraged; a private repo needs access granted to
   Colosseum's review address).
7. A two-to-three-minute presentation video (judges review it first).
8. A product demo video of no more than three minutes explaining how the product works.
9. Go-to-market strategy, demand validation and distribution plans.

Also from the FAQ:
- Pre-existing code is allowed, but all relevant past development work must be disclosed in the
  submission form; products are judged on work done between the start and end dates.
- One submission per team; the team leader submits before the deadline.
- Weekly updates are optional but recommended: a concise one-minute video on the past week's
  progress and challenges.
- Judging factors: founder and market fit, insight, product and execution, market size, founder
  communication, viability, traction.
